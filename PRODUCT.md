# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **Supplier (Koperasi Atsiri):** Cooperatives consolidating patchouli oil and circular waste co-products from rural Aceh farmers. Basic digital literacy, sensitive to payout times, seeking objective quality grading, fair pricing, and simple monetization of agricultural waste.
- **Buyer Lokal (UMKM Parfum & Organik):** Digital-native cosmetic/perfume makers and agricultural buyers. Seeking 100% pure patchouli oil (to protect scent formulas) and verified circular organic inputs (e.g., hydrosol for skincare base, compost/biochar for organic cultivation).
- **Buyer Global (Fragrance Houses & Sustainable Sourcing):** Sourcing agents strictly governed by ESG, EUDR, and international compliance. Seeking export documents (MSDS), GPS traceability of raw materials, and circular economy credentials.
- **Admin (Tim Valam):** Operational staff managing collection hubs, verifying GC-MS chemical reports, inputs lab data, and moderating transactions for both oils and circular products.

## Product Purpose
A B2B managed-marketplace for patchouli oil and zero-waste circular co-products. Acts as a "trust infrastructure" that digitizes physical quality data (Certificate of Analysis) and maps hulu supply chains to eliminate quality fraud, middleman price exploitation, and upcycle agricultural waste into valuable secondary inputs.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, Analytical, Institutional, and Sustainable (Terpercaya, Analitis, Institusional, Berkelanjutan). Promotes expert confidence, supply chain certainty, and zero-waste eco-efficiency.

## Anti-references
- **Generic consumer e-commerce:** Omit heavy illustrations, consumer shopping banners, and lifestyle marketing.
- **SaaS clichés:** Refuse text gradients, glassmorphism default cards, and tiny uppercase eyebrows/eyecatchers on every single section.
- **Middleman opacity:** Omit opaque pricing, hidden testing data, and hidden supplier source names.

## Design Principles
- **Data Over Decoration:** Prioritize chemical specs (PA%, moisture) and verification badges over lifestyle photography or visual flair.
- **Transparency First:** Ensure interactive digital CoAs and GPS terroir maps are open, searchable, and prominent.
- **Frictionless Hulu:** Keep the supplier dashboard simple, using wizards that hide advanced chemical terms.
- **Enterprise Procurement:** Use dense data tables, clear status hierarchies, and standard procurement fields (RFQ, Lead Time).
- **Eco-Sirkular Distinctiveness:** Clearly distinguish circular products (compost, biochar, hydrosol) from essential oils using custom icons (e.g., Leaf), dedicated pricing units (e.g., Sack, Jerigen), and separate filtering criteria to avoid mixups.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance.
- Responsive mobile viewports for rural cooperatives using basic smartphones.
- High contrast text targets (>= 4.5:1).
- Supports reduced-motion overrides (`@media prefers-reduced-motion`).
